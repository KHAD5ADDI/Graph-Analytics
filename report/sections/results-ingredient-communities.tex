Louvain finds five communities in the ingredient graph (modularity $0.400$
with seed 42). Table~\ref{tab:communities} lists the ingredients with the
highest strength in each community, and Figure~\ref{fig:ingredients} shows
the graph. Community 0 (112 ingredients) contains vegetables, herbs and
mushrooms; community 1 (97) sweet and baking ingredients; community 2 (57)
salad, sauce and condiment ingredients together with beef and pork; community 3
(55) ginger, soy sauce, fish, lime and coconut milk; and community 4 (41)
spices and legumes. These descriptions summarise the listed ingredients. We did
not compare the communities with the cuisine labels, so we do not call them
cuisines.

\paragraph{Stability over seeds.}
Over 30 seeds, the number of communities ranges from 4 to 6 and equals 5 in 28
runs. The modularity ranges from $0.389$ to $0.402$, and it is $0.400$ for
seed 42. Ingredients that share a community in at least 27 of the 30 runs form
13 groups, which cover 349 of the 362 ingredients; the five largest groups
have 109, 95, 44, 43 and 39 ingredients. Table~\ref{tab:stability} shows how
much of each seed-42 community lies in such a group. Communities 1 and 4 are
almost entirely stable, while community 2 is the least stable, with 39 of its
57 ingredients in a stable group. For the country graph, the number of
communities ranges from 9 to 11 (11 in 21 runs) and the modularity from $0.271$
to $0.288$; 14 groups cover 154 of the 171 countries. Stability over seeds shows
that the partition is not an accident of one seed. It does not show that no
better partition exists, because Louvain is a heuristic.
