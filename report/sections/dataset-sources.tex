\paragraph{CulinaryDB.}
CulinaryDB~\cite{culinarydb}, from the Complex Systems Laboratory (CoSyLab)
at IIIT-Delhi, is distributed as four CSV files under a CC BY-NC-SA 3.0
licence. We use this readily downloadable bundle. It contains 45{,}772
recipes, each with a cuisine label (26 labels), and the data providers have
mapped the ingredient names used in the recipes to a list of ingredients. This
mapping gives 456{,}279 recipe--ingredient pairs, or 412{,}560 once we remove
duplicate pairs. In all, 695 distinct ingredients occur in recipes, among them
95 of the 103 compound ingredients, such as garam masala. Of the 45{,}772
recipes, 45{,}749 have at least one mapped ingredient. The sample is
unbalanced: 16{,}118 recipes carry the label USA and 7{,}504 the label Italy,
while the smallest labels have 14 and 15 recipes. We use the cuisine labels
neither to build the graphs nor to evaluate the communities.

\paragraph{National dishes.}
The dataset of Wunderlich and Kohler~\cite{wunderlich2022data}, described in
a data paper~\cite{wunderlich2022paper}, is published on EnviDat under the
ODbL licence with the Database Contents License. It lists dishes of 171
countries and territories, with 218 binary ingredient indicators, and was
collected between 4 June and 23 August 2018. The file uses semicolons as
separators and the cp1252 encoding. It contains one malformed row, without
country or dish and with a stray value (58) in one ingredient column; we
discard it. The 350 remaining dishes are the 171 dishes that the file flags as
the national dish of their country (one per country) and 179 further dishes.

\paragraph{UN regions.}
To place countries in regions, we use the United Nations (UN) region and
sub-region of each country from a public table~\cite{unregions}, licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0 (version of 19 June 2024). We join it to the dishes through
ISO 3166 alpha-3 codes, never through country names. Two codes in the dishes
file are obsolete (ZAR and ROM) and we map them to COD and ROU. All 171
countries obtain a region. We use the regions only to describe the country
communities.

The raw files are in the project's GitHub repository
(\url{https://github.com/KHAD5ADDI/Graph-Analytics}) with their original
licences.
